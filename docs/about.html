About

I'm Maria Providencia Castro.

Data analyst turned integrative health practitioner. Thirteen years of reading patterns in numbers, and more than a decade of reading them in people.

[Portrait of Maria]

My story

People rarely come to me short on information. They come to me short on someone who will look at the whole picture.

My first career was in data. For thirteen years, including at a Fortune 500 travel technology company, I organized complex systems and looked for the patterns hiding in them. I didn't know it yet, but I was training for the work I do now.

In 2013 I stepped toward health. I began my formal training at the American College of Healthcare Sciences and opened a private practice, working one-on-one with clients and leading workshops in my community. The pattern I saw again and again: symptoms that seemed unrelated almost never were.

From there I went to where people actually make health decisions: the supplement aisle. I started as a nutritional health coach on the retail floor of a national natural and organic grocery chain and went on to lead the nutritional health coaching program across five states, with a team of twenty-two coaches. I learned products from the inside, from the people who make them, and from thousands of real conversations.

Then I worked inside the healthcare system as a community health worker for one of the nation's largest nonprofit health systems, helping people navigate care across hospital networks. And as a senior health coach in a national remote program, I supported 80 to 120 clients at a time, through structured, months-long work.

Along the way I went back to school and earned my Bachelor of Science in Integrative Health Sciences in 2023. Get Real Integrative Health is where all of it comes together: an analyst's eye, a practitioner's training, and more than a decade of listening closely.

How I practice

Signals over symptoms

A symptom is where the story starts, not where it ends. I look for what connects them.

Understanding over dependence

You should leave every session knowing more about your own body than when you arrived.

Invitation over pressure

No hard sells and no scare tactics. When you're ready, I'm here.

Evidence over hype

I tell you what is well supported, what is promising, and what is still marketing. You deserve to know the difference.

Regulate before you mobilize

A body under strain can't make big changes well. We steady the system first, then ask it to do more.
