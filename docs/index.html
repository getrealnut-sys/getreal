A different kind of practice

Most care asks what treats the symptom. I ask what's driving it.

Fatigue, stubborn weight, blood sugar that won't settle, stress that lives in your body. These rarely come from one place. They're connected, and they send signals.

I spent thirteen years as a data analyst before I ever worked in health, and I still think like one. I look at your history, your habits, and your labs together, find the pattern underneath, and build a plan you can actually live with.

My commitments

Two promises I make to every client.

Evidence-honest

Well supported, promising, or still marketing.

The wellness world is loud with overclaims. I tell you which category a recommendation falls into, so you can decide with clear eyes. My private clinical reference library, cross-checked against the research, is built to keep me honest.

Regulate before you mobilize

Steady first. Then change.

A body under strain can't make big changes well. Before cleanses, protocols, or anything aggressive, we calm and support the system, so the work that follows can actually hold.
