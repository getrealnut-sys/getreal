Before you start

Everyone is telling you to add something. Another supplement. Another protocol. Another practitioner. Another test.

What if the first move is to take something away?

Your body is not broken. It is backlogged. When a system is overwhelmed, you do not add more inputs. You reduce the load and let it stabilize. That is not alternative medicine. That is systems engineering applied to physiology.

This is a 7-day protocol. One action per day. No purchases required. No new supplements. No apps to download. Just observation, subtraction, and your own body's response.

One important rule

Do not pause or change anything your doctor or provider prescribed, including medications, prescribed supplements, and prescribed skin treatments. This protocol is only for the extras you chose on your own.

Day 1

The Inventory

Action

Write down everything you consume, apply, or expose yourself to in a typical day.

Not just food. Everything.

  • What you eat and drink (including supplements, vitamins, teas, powders)
  • What you put on your skin (lotions, sunscreen, makeup, soap, deodorant)
  • What you breathe (candles, air fresheners, cleaning products, diffusers)
  • What you scroll (news, social media, group chats, email volume)
  • What you listen to (podcasts, music, background noise, silence)

Do not judge the list. Just make it. Your body already knows what is on it. Now your conscious mind does too.

Write it down. Paper or phone. Just get it out of your head.

Day 2

Identify the Noise

Action

Look at your Day 1 list. Circle anything that was not there 12 months ago.

These are the variables your body is still adjusting to. Some might be beneficial. Some might be adding load without benefit. The point is not to eliminate them. The point is to see them.

Now ask: which of these did I add because someone recommended it, and which did I add because my body asked for it?

There is a difference.

Day 3

Remove One Ingestible

Action

Pick one supplement, powder, tea, or non-essential item you consume daily and pause it for the remaining 5 days. Nothing prescribed.

Choose the one you feel the least certain about. The one you take because you "should" but have never noticed a clear difference from taking it.

Do not replace it with something else. That defeats the purpose. The body cannot tell you what it thinks about the removal if you immediately substitute.

Note how you feel tonight and tomorrow morning. Write one sentence.

Day 4

Remove One Topical

Action

Pick one product you apply to your skin daily and pause it for the remaining 4 days. Nothing prescribed, and keep your sunscreen.

Your skin is an organ. It absorbs what you put on it. Every product is an input your body processes. Reducing inputs gives your system one less thing to manage.

Choose something you use out of habit rather than necessity.

Note any changes: skin feel, dryness, oiliness, breakouts, nothing at all. Write one sentence.

Day 5

Remove One Hour of Input

Action

Identify one hour in your day that is filled with noise and make it silent.

This could be:

  • The first hour after waking (no phone, no email, no news)
  • The last hour before sleep (no screens, no scrolling)
  • A commute without podcasts or music
  • A meal without background content

Your nervous system processes everything you expose it to. Silence is not empty. It is space for your system to process the backlog.

Note what your mind does with the space. Write one sentence.

Day 6

Observe

Action

Change nothing today. Just observe.

You have removed three inputs over the past three days. Today you pay attention to what your body is doing with the extra bandwidth.

Questions to sit with:

  • Is my energy different at any point in the day?
  • Am I sleeping differently?
  • Is my digestion different?
  • Am I more or less anxious?
  • Did I notice anything I was not expecting?

Write your observations. No judgment. Just data.

Day 7

The Signal

Action

Review your notes from Days 3 through 6. Ask one question: did my body notice?

If you removed something and nothing changed, that item may not be serving you. It is occupying space in your routine without contributing.

If you removed something and something shifted, that is a signal. Whether positive or negative, your body just told you that input matters. Now you know.

This is how investigation works. Not adding until something helps. Removing until something speaks.

What you just did

You did not do a cleanse. You did not start a protocol. You did not buy anything.

You listened. You subtracted. You observed.

That is the first step in how I work: stabilize the system before you ask it to change. Most people skip this step entirely. You just completed it.

The Subtraction Protocol is the beginning of a longer investigation. Your body has patterns it has been trying to show you. The next guide helps you read them.
