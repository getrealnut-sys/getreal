How to prepare

Get the most from your first session

Your session time should go to reading the picture, not assembling it. Three things make the biggest difference, and the short intake form you'll fill out after booking walks you through them.

Bring the bottles.

Every supplement, medication, and remedy you take, including the ones you take now and then or quietly stopped. Your list is often the first clue.

Timing is data.

A symptom at 2 a.m. tells a different story than one after lunch. When things get better or worse matters as much as what happens.

Notice, don't interpret.

Bring what you've observed, not what you think it means. Reading the pattern is my job. Yours is to tell me what you see.
